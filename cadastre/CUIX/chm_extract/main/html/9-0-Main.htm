COM Interface Usage

The ZWCAD COM components play a unique role. They can be invoked by ZWCAD.NET to utilize their well-encapsulated interfaces, or they can be driven by standalone programs to launch ZWCAD, thereby enabling automation functionalities. This flexibility allows developers to integrate and extend ZWCAD's capabilities more efficiently, meeting diverse development requirements.
