Rule Redefinition

Rule Redefinition is an alternative solution in ZWCAD.NET development for the lack of custom entity functionality. It allows displaying certain standard entities in alternative forms to meet specific design requirements. Through Rule Redefinition, developers can flexibly adjust the display methods or behaviors of existing entities, providing greater convenience and extensibility during the development process.
